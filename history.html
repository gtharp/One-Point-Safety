Featured: The Flying Wedge

Football's most notorious formation: two wings of players, already running at full speed, converge into a V aimed at one point on the defensive line — with the ball carrier tucked inside, shielded by the mass. First used by Harvard against Yale in 1892 (not Princeton, a common mix-up). Reconstructed from historical accounts, not a single primary diagram — see "Read more" for sourcing and caveats.
